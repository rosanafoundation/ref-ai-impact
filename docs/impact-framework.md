# REF AI Impact Initiative - Impact Framework

## Purpose

The REF AI Impact Initiative will measure how artificial intelligence and digital solutions improve programme delivery, accountability, and community impact.

The framework will guide monitoring, evaluation, learning, and continuous improvement.

## Expected Outcomes

### Outcome 1: Improved Programme Management

Indicators:

- Number of beneficiaries digitally registered.
- Percentage improvement in data organization.
- Reduction in manual reporting processes.

---

### Outcome 2: Better Decision-Making Through Data

Indicators:

- Availability of real-time programme information.
- Number of reports generated using digital tools.
- Use of AI-supported analysis for programme decisions.

---

### Outcome 3: Improved Community Support Services

Indicators:

- Number of women, children, and vulnerable households reached.
- Improved tracking of education and nutrition interventions.
- Increased responsiveness to beneficiary needs.

---

### Outcome 4: Stronger Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL)

Indicators:

- Improved programme tracking.
- Faster reporting cycles.
- Better documentation of lessons learned.

## Monitoring Approach

REF will use:

- Digital data collection.
- Beneficiary feedback mechanisms.
- Programme dashboards.
- Periodic assessments.
- Staff and stakeholder reviews.

## Learning and Adaptation

The project will use collected data to:

- Improve programme strategies.
- Identify emerging community needs.
- Strengthen future interventions.
- Share learning with partners.

## Long-Term Impact

The initiative aims to demonstrate how responsible AI adoption can strengthen nonprofit organizations and improve social impact delivery in Nigeria.
