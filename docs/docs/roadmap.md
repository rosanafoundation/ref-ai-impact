# REF AI Impact Initiative - Project Roadmap

## Overview

The REF AI Impact Initiative will be developed through a gradual implementation roadmap that moves from planning and testing to deployment and scaling.

## Phase 1: Foundation and Planning (2026)

Key Activities:

- Define AI use cases for REF programmes.
- Review existing data management processes.
- Identify technology requirements.
- Engage stakeholders and partners.
- Develop implementation strategies.

Expected Results:

- Clear AI adoption plan.
- Identified priority solutions.
- Prepared implementation framework.

---

## Phase 2: Digital System Development

Key Activities:

- Develop beneficiary management tools.
- Establish digital data collection systems.
- Create monitoring dashboards.
- Develop AI-assisted reporting features.

Expected Results:

- Improved programme information systems.
- Better data accessibility and reporting.

---

## Phase 3: Pilot Implementation

Key Activities:

- Test AI solutions within selected REF programmes.
- Train staff and users.
- Collect feedback.
- Improve system functionality.

Expected Results:

- Tested and refined AI tools.
- Increased staff digital capacity.

---

## Phase 4: Expansion and Partnership Development

Key Activities:

- Expand successful solutions.
- Build partnerships with technology organizations.
- Share learning and best practices.
- Explore funding opportunities.

Expected Results:

- Wider adoption of AI solutions.
- Stronger collaboration network.

---

## Phase 5: Long-Term Sustainability

Key Activities:

- Continuous improvement of AI systems.
- Staff capacity development.
- Integration into REF operations.
- Innovation and research.

Expected Results:

- Sustainable AI-powered social impact model.

## Success Vision

The long-term goal is to establish REF as an organization demonstrating how responsible AI can strengthen nonprofit operations and improve outcomes for vulnerable communities.
