# REF AI Impact Initiative - Partnership Brief

## Introduction

The REF AI Impact Initiative seeks collaboration with technology organizations, development partners, research institutions, and funding organizations interested in applying Artificial Intelligence for social impact.

The initiative combines community development experience with emerging technologies to improve programme effectiveness and accountability.

## Partnership Opportunities

REF welcomes partnerships in the following areas:

### Technology Partnerships

Support areas:

- AI solution development.
- Digital platforms.
- Data management systems.
- Dashboard development.
- Technical expertise.

### Research Partnerships

Support areas:

- AI impact studies.
- Innovation research.
- Evidence generation.
- Knowledge sharing.

### Funding Partnerships

Support areas:

- Pilot implementation.
- Technology development.
- Capacity building.
- Community expansion.

### Capacity Building Partnerships

Support areas:

- Staff digital skills training.
- AI literacy.
- Data management training.
- Innovation workshops.

## Areas of Impact

The initiative focuses on:

- Women empowerment.
- Education support.
- Nutrition improvement.
- Community health.
- Digital inclusion.
- Evidence-based programming.

## Why Partner With REF

Partners will contribute to:

- Advancing responsible AI adoption in Nigeria.
- Strengthening nonprofit innovation.
- Supporting vulnerable communities.
- Demonstrating practical AI solutions for development challenges.

## About REF

Rosana Empowerment Foundation (REF) is a Nigerian nonprofit organization committed to empowering vulnerable communities through sustainable development, innovation, advocacy, and evidence-based programming.
