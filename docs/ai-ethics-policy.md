# REF AI Impact Initiative - AI Ethics and Data Protection Policy

## Overview

The REF AI Impact Initiative is committed to the responsible, ethical, and human-centred use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to support community development.

Artificial Intelligence will be applied as a tool to strengthen decision-making, improve programme delivery, and increase social impact while protecting the rights, dignity, and privacy of beneficiaries.

## Responsible AI Principles

## 1. Human-Centred Approach

AI tools developed through this initiative will support human decision-making and will not replace the important role of programme staff, community leaders, and beneficiaries.

Human oversight will remain central to all important decisions.

## 2. Data Privacy and Protection

REF will prioritize the protection of personal and programme information through:

- Secure data storage.
- Controlled access to information.
- Responsible data collection practices.
- Protection of beneficiary confidentiality.
- Appropriate use of collected information.

## 3. Transparency

REF will promote transparency by ensuring that:

- Users understand how AI tools support programmes.
- AI-generated information is reviewed before use.
- Decisions affecting communities remain explainable.

## 4. Fairness and Inclusion

The initiative will work to prevent unfair outcomes by:

- Considering diverse community needs.
- Avoiding discrimination.
- Ensuring vulnerable groups benefit equally.
- Regularly reviewing AI performance.

## 5. Data Accuracy and Quality

REF will promote reliable AI outcomes through:

- Accurate data collection.
- Regular data review.
- Continuous improvement of digital systems.
- Verification of AI-supported recommendations.

## Beneficiary Protection

The initiative will ensure that:

- Participation is voluntary.
- Beneficiaries are informed about digital systems being used.
- Sensitive information receives appropriate protection.
- Technology does not create barriers to accessing services.

## AI Governance

REF will establish appropriate oversight mechanisms including:

- Staff responsible for monitoring AI applications.
- Regular review of AI performance.
- Feedback mechanisms for users and beneficiaries.
- Continuous improvement of systems.

## Commitment

REF believes that responsible AI can strengthen social development when applied ethically, inclusively, and transparently.

The organization is committed to using technology to advance dignity, empowerment, and sustainable impact.
